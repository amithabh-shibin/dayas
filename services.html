Immigration & Residency Solutions

Find out which skilled migration, permanent residency or family visa options you may be able to apply for.

International Work & Employment

We can help you prepare an overseas job application and understand the steps for a work permit or employer-sponsored visa.

Global Mobility & Corporate Immigration

Moving staff to another country? We can help with corporate visas, employee documents and relocation plans.

Tell us where your staff are going, how many people are moving and when they need to start. We can help plan what your team and the receiving office need to prepare.

What to have ready

List the departure and destination countries, the number of employees, the assignment dates and whether family members will travel. Include the name of the team coordinating the move on your side.

How we can help

  • International employee mobility
  • Corporate immigration coordination
  • Business relocation assistance
  • Cross-border workforce planning
  • Corporate visa and documentation support
  • Employer mobility solutions

International Recruitment & Talent Solutions

We help employers recruit skilled tradespeople, operational staff and professionals for overseas roles.

We can help write the role brief, screen candidates and arrange interviews. If you’re hiring a larger team, we can discuss trade tests and whether it makes sense for staff to start in groups.

What to have ready

Send a role brief with the required experience, number of hires, work location and start date. Flag any trade tests or qualifications the employer wants to check during screening.

How we can help

  • Blue-collar and skilled workforce sourcing
  • Professional recruitment
  • Bulk recruitment and manpower supply coordination
  • Candidate pre-screening
  • Trade testing where required
  • Interview and employer coordination
  • Recruitment process support

Education & Visitor Immigration

Get help with university or college applications, student visas and visits for tourism, business or family.

For study, tell us where you’d like to apply and when you want to start. For a visit, tell us who’s travelling and why so we can explain what paperwork you may need.

Getting started

How to get started

Tell us what you need

Let us know why you’re getting in touch, the country involved and your target dates. If you’re hiring, include the roles and start dates.

Review the requirements

We’ll talk through the paperwork and whether you may need services like translation, attestation or candidate screening.

Review the plan and costs

Before you decide, we’ll put the proposed work, estimated timing and charges in writing.

Planning an application

Three questions to ask before you apply

A useful plan covers the purpose of the move, the documents available and any deadlines you already know about.

Who is involved?

Tell us if the application is for you, your family or an employee. If an employer or university is involved, explain what they’ve already provided.

What have you prepared?

List the documents you already have and flag any that might take time to obtain. If you’ve applied before, let us know where things stand.

What date are you working towards?

Share your intended travel, enrolment or start date. If it’s flexible, tell us which dates you could consider.
