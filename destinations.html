What to consider when choosing a destination

Purpose and eligibility

Work, study, family sponsorship and long-term residency require different evidence. Start by identifying the purpose of the move and the qualifications, offers or sponsorship you can document.

Budget and timing

Consider application costs, document preparation, travel and arrival expenses. Share your target date so the advisory desk can discuss which milestones may affect your plans.

Family and arrival plans

If family will join you, ask about dependant documentation and whether applications can be coordinated. Employers can also discuss relocation dates and onboarding needs.
