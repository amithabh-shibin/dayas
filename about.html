About us

From Dubai to your next destination

Dayas Group brings immigration, recruitment, education, residency and mobility services together under one advisory desk.

Our company

Established in Dubai in 2016

Based in Creek Tower, Deira, Dayas Group supports individuals, families and organisations with international moves. The company profile identifies Dayas Group as a government-registered and licensed entity in Dubai.

Each engagement starts with a review of your goals and the available routes. The Corporate Advisory Desk coordinates documentation, filing and mobilisation with a single point of contact.

Our vision

To be a trusted regional partner for people and organisations pursuing opportunities across borders.

Our mission

To deliver clear, compliant immigration, recruitment and mobility support, from the initial assessment through the agreed scope of work.

How we work

Our commitments

  1. Eligibility first

    We review whether a route fits your circumstances before recommending it.

  2. Documented compliance

    We prepare evidence according to the requirements of the receiving authority.

  3. Clear commercial terms

    We explain scope, fees and expected third-party costs in writing.

  4. Confidentiality

    We handle personal and financial records as part of the agreed process.

  5. Support after approval

    Where agreed, we assist with arrival, onboarding and dependant processing.

Your client journey

What to expect when you engage us

  1. Eligibility assessment

    We review your goals, credentials and circumstances against the criteria for potential routes.

  2. Route and written terms

    You receive the proposed scope, estimated timeline and a breakdown of professional, government and third-party costs.

  3. Documentation

    We help collect and verify evidence and coordinate translation or legalisation where required.

  4. Filing and case management

    Your assigned contact coordinates submission and provides updates as the case progresses.

  5. Arrival and settlement

    Where included in your scope, we help coordinate arrival, onboarding and dependant support.
