About us

Immigration and recruitment advice for international moves

We help people move, work and study abroad. We also help employers recruit and relocate staff internationally.

Our company

Who we are

Dayas Group is an international immigration, global mobility and recruitment consultancy headquartered in the UAE. We’ve worked in immigration and overseas recruitment since 2016.

Our clients include people applying for jobs and visas abroad, companies hiring overseas and recruitment partners working across countries.

Some clients come to us with an offer or a destination in mind. Others are still comparing options. We start with the information you have and help you decide what to look into next.

How we work

Our core values

  1. Integrity and transparency

    We explain your options, what we can help with and what it costs before you decide.

  2. Professional responsibility

    You’ll know who to contact if you have a question or need an update.

  3. Client-focused service

    We start by asking what matters to you and what you’re trying to achieve.

  4. Global collaboration

    We work with employers and recruitment partners in different countries.

  5. Compliance and accountability

    We check what the application requires and make clear who handles each step.

  6. Long-term relationships

    We keep in touch with clients and partners as the work progresses.

Working with us

How we work with you

  1. We look at your options

    We ask about your plans and qualifications, then review which options might suit you.

  2. We explain the plan and costs

    You get a written outline of the work, the estimated timing, our fees and any government or third-party charges.

  3. Documentation

    We help you gather and check the paperwork. If translations or legalisation are required, we can help arrange them.

  4. We help with the submission

    Your contact helps coordinate the submission and keeps you updated.

  5. Arrival and settlement

    If arrival support is part of your agreement, we can help with travel plans, getting settled or paperwork for family members.

  6. We review what comes next

    If plans change, let your contact know. We can discuss how a new date, an updated offer or a request for more documents affects the work we’ve agreed to do.

How we advise

What we consider before suggesting a route

A good first conversation starts with the practical details. We look at why you want to move, what you can document and what needs to happen before your target date.

Your plans

Tell us whether you want to work, study, join family or hire overseas. If your plans are flexible, explain what matters most so we can discuss which questions to answer first.

Your paperwork

A CV, a job offer or an admission letter can give us a clearer starting point. Tell us what you already have so we can discuss what else may be needed.

Your timing

Let us know about deadlines for an offer, enrolment or work assignment. That helps us discuss which steps to prepare first.
