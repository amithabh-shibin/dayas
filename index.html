Discuss your plans
What happens in the first consultation?

The advisory desk asks about your destination, plans and background, then identifies what information is needed to assess suitable options.

How much will my application cost?

Costs depend on the route and the documentation involved. The proposed scope and the professional, government and third-party charges are outlined in writing before engagement.

Can Dayas Group help with a spouse or children?

Yes. The team can assess sponsorship or dependant options and explain the documents required for the destination you are considering.

Can employers request support for multiple hires?

Yes. The recruitment practice handles individual searches, specialist contract roles and larger workforce mobilisation assignments.

Who decides the outcome of a visa application?

The relevant authority makes the final decision. Timing depends on the route, completeness of the evidence and the authority’s processing schedule.

Can you help me shortlist universities?

Yes. The team can help compare programmes based on academic background, study plans and budget, then guide you through admissions and visa documentation.
