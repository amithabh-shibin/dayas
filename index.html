FAQ

Frequently asked questions

If your question is about a particular country or a family member, get in touch and tell us a little about your plans.

Discuss your plans
What happens in the first consultation?

We’ll ask where you want to go, why you’re moving and what stage you’re at. That helps us see what to look into first.

How much will my application cost?

It depends on the application and the documents you need. Before you decide, we’ll explain our fees and any government or third-party charges in writing.

Can Dayas Group help with a spouse or children?

Yes. Tell us who plans to travel with you and where you’re going. We can explain the family visa options and paperwork that may apply.

Can employers request support for multiple hires?

Yes. We can help fill one role, find specialist contractors or recruit a larger team.

Who decides the outcome of a visa application?

The immigration authority makes the decision. Processing times vary by application type and by how long the authority takes to review your documents.

Can you help me shortlist universities?

Yes. Tell us what you want to study, your qualifications and your budget. We can help you compare courses and prepare your applications.

Can I get in touch before I choose a country?

Yes. Tell us what you hope to do abroad, whether anyone will move with you and when you’d like to go. If you have a shortlist, share it so we can discuss what to check first.

What if I already started my application?

Let us know what you’ve submitted and whether you’ve received a request for more information. Include any deadline in your first message so we can discuss what help you need.
