Before you get in touch

What to include in your first enquiry

Individuals and families

Tell us where you want to move, why, when you hope to go and who’s travelling with you.

Students

Share what you’ve studied so far, which course interests you, your budget and when you’d like to start.

Employers

Tell us the role, work location, number of hires, project duration and planned start date.

After your first enquiry

What happens next

We ask about your plans

We may ask a few questions about your destination, travel dates, work experience or hiring plans.

We explain the next steps

We’ll explain what we need to review your enquiry and discuss what we can help with and what it may cost.
